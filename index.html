Laws that protect animals are on the books. How far they are applied is rarely measured.

Egyptian law, by animal

Each category of animal falls under a different law, which punishes a different act with a different penalty.

  • A dangerous animalPossessing, trading or breeding it in breach of the lawA fine of EGP 10,000 to 500,000 for each animalLaw No. 29 of 2023, Articles 2 and 15
  • A protected wild animalKilling, capturing, possessing or trading itImprisonment and a fine of EGP 5,000 to 50,000, or either, with confiscation in every caseEnvironment Law No. 4 of 1994, Articles 28 and 84
  • A horse, donkey or head of livestockDeliberately killing it without necessityImprisonment with labourPenal Code, Article 355
  • A dog or a catDeliberately killing it without necessity, or poisoning itImprisonment of up to six months or a fine of up to EGP 200Penal Code, Article 357

As written in the law. October 2026.
